# Brief

A short showreel for sift, cut for the README.

## Stated
- 16:9, ~25 s, minimal and elegant, showreel pacing.
- Showcase every feature.
- Just build it: no storyboard stop.
- The interface on screen is a designed terminal mock, built from captures of the
  real binary rather than invented UI.
- Explain what each beat does in words.
- Sound.

## Inferred / defaulted
- No voiceover: a terminal film carries its own words (`sound.md` §1).
- Music bed: none available without a HeyGen sign-in; sound design only (key
  presses, typing, three whooshes, one impact) from the bundled Pixabay library.
- Body font JetBrains Mono (bundled), 108 x 29 character grid scaled 0.9 into a
  1728 x 972 stage so a caption band fits under it.
- Captions are the film's own copy, not the app's: the app's UI text is never
  altered.
